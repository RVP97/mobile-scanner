Point your camera at any code and Lunet tells you what it is, where it really goes and what to do next. A QR code reader and barcode scanner built around safety. No account, no ads, no tracking.

CHECKED BEFORE YOU TAP
QR phishing is on the rise: a sticker on a parking meter, a fake menu, a “missed delivery” card. Lunet inspects every link before anything opens.
- Follows redirects to show the real destination, not the one printed on the sign
- Spots lookalike domains, like n0rthbank.com posing as northbank.com
- Flags brand-new domains, hidden @-tricks, link shorteners and insecure pages
- Gives a clear verdict: Safe, Be careful or Danger, with reasons in plain words
- For dangerous codes, Don't Open is the default

THE RIGHT ANSWER FOR EVERY CODE
Lunet reads 19 formats, including QR, EAN, UPC, Code 128, PDF417, Aztec and Data Matrix, and knows what each one is for.
- Wi-Fi: join the network in one tap
- Boarding passes: add to Apple Wallet or your calendar
- Contacts and events: save them straight to your iPhone
- Products: look up ingredients and details
- Packages: track a delivery
- Email, messages, maps and crypto addresses, each handled the right way
Scan from photos and screenshots, or catch several codes at once with multi-scan.

YOUR CODES, ONE TAP AWAY
Keep your home Wi-Fi, your contact card and upcoming boarding passes as Wallet-style cards on the Home screen. One tap shows the code full screen at maximum brightness, ready for the gate or a friend's phone.
History remembers everything you scan, with search, filters and pins. Lock it with Face ID, export it as CSV and, if you choose, see where each code was scanned.

MAKE BEAUTIFUL CODES
Create codes for Wi-Fi, links, contacts, text, email, SMS, phone calls, events, locations and product barcodes in 15 symbologies. In Studio, style QR codes with custom dots, corners, colours, gradients, a logo and frames, then let Lunet verify on device that your design still scans.

ALWAYS WITHIN REACH
Start a scan from Control Centre, the Lock Screen, the Action button, a widget or Siri. Multi-scan shows its progress in a Live Activity, and Shortcuts can scan codes inside any image.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your history never leaves your device. Lunet goes online only when it has to: to check a link with that link's own server, to look up a product on Open Food Facts, or to sign a pass you add to Apple Wallet. Nothing is stored and nothing is tracked.

Built natively for iPhone and iPad, with Liquid Glass, light and dark appearance, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver.
