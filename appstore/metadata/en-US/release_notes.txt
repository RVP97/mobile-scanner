- Your codes: keep your Wi-Fi, contact card and passes on Home, shown full screen in one tap
- Studio: design QR codes with custom styles, colors, logos and frames
- Scan from Control Center, the Lock Screen, the Action button, widgets and Siri
- A fresh design with Liquid Glass, dark mode and full accessibility
